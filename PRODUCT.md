# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

OEC Verify serves overseas Filipino workers submitting or checking an application, and the staff evaluators who review submissions and issue decisions. Applicants need a clear, reliable path from submission to receipt; evaluators need an efficient, auditable workspace for handling decisions.

## Product Purpose

The product collects application information, provides time-limited verification receipts, and gives authorized staff the tools to verify, reject, revoke, and export records. Success means applicants receive dependable verification without unnecessary delay while staff can make accurate, traceable decisions.

## Positioning

OEC Verify keeps application verification moving efficiently without sacrificing the trust and traceability expected from an official service.

## Brand Personality

Precise and efficient. The service should feel official, calm, and easy to act on: it explains the current state, the next step, and the reason for each decision without ceremony or ambiguity.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful startup styling, gamified interactions, casual language, and trend-led visual decoration that can weaken confidence in an official verification workflow.

## Design Principles

- Make every status, requirement, and next action immediately legible.
- Prioritize fast, accurate work for applicants and evaluators equally.
- Treat trust as a product behavior: use clear language, auditable decisions, and consistent feedback.
- Preserve a calm, task-focused interface even when a submission needs attention.
- Prefer familiar, accessible controls over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across public and evaluator interfaces. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, text alternatives, reduced-motion support, and status communication that never relies on color alone.
